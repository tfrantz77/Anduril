Create a signature in Gmail
===========================

Add your name and contact information automatically to new email messages
you send from Gmail in a desktop web browser.

Before you begin
----------------

Go to `Gmail <https://mail.google.com/>`_ and sign in to the account you
want to use. These instructions use the English interface.

Create your signature
---------------------

1. In the upper-right corner, select **Settings** (the gear icon), and
   then select **See all settings**.
2. On the **General** tab, scroll to **Signature**, and then select
   **Create new**.
3. Enter a name for your signature, such as **Work**, and then select
   **Create**. This name identifies the signature in your settings; it
   isn't the text that appears in your messages.
4. In the text box next to the signature name, enter the information you
   want to include, such as your name, job title, and phone number.

   To change the appearance of your text, use the formatting options
   below the text box.

Set when your signature appears
-------------------------------

1. Under **Signature defaults**, in **FOR NEW EMAILS USE**, select the
   signature you created.
2. To include the signature in replies and forwarded messages, select
   it in **ON REPLY/FORWARD USE**. Otherwise, select **No signature**.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the page, and then select **Save Changes**.

Check your signature
--------------------

1. Select **Compose** to start a new message.
2. Confirm that your signature appears in the message and that the
   information is correct. You don't need to send the message.

If the signature doesn't appear, return to **Signature defaults** and
confirm that your signature is selected in **FOR NEW EMAILS USE**.
Select **Save Changes**, and then start another new message.
